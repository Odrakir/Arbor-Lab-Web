Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Send me an email. I read everything and usually reply within a couple of days.

Which devices are supported?

iPad and iPhone running iPadOS 26 or iOS 26 or later.

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

What format do 3D exports use?

USDZ, which opens in Quick Look, Reality Composer and most 3D tools. Use the share button to save it to Files or send it to another app.

Reporting a bug

Include your device, iOS version, app version and, if you can, the rules that caused the problem.

Privacy

Effective 4 November 2026

The short version: Arbor Lab doesn’t collect any personal data.

  • No accounts, no tracking. There’s no sign-up, no analytics and no advertising. Nothing you make leaves your device unless you share or export it yourself.
  • Your creations stay on your device. Rules, trees and exports are stored locally (and in your iCloud, if you use iCloud backup), not on any server of mine.
  • Camera use for AR. When you use AR, the camera feed is processed on your device by Apple’s ARKit to place the tree. It isn’t recorded or sent anywhere. Photos you take are saved only where you choose.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. Pro and tips go through the App Store. I never see your payment details; Apple’s privacy policy applies to those transactions.
  • Email. If you write to support, I use your address only to reply, and don’t share it.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions? Use the email in Support.
